Free OSINT tools online

A searchable directory of open source intelligence (OSINT) tools you can use in your browser: reverse image search, username and email lookup, WHOIS and DNS, Shodan, satellite maps, flight tracking and fact-checking. No install needed.

Frameworks and directories

  • OSINT Framework: Clickable tree of tools by category
  • Bellingcat Toolkit: Curated investigation tools with notes
  • Cyber Detective: Large categorized collection
  • Start.me OSINT: Dashboard of hundreds of links

Search engines and archives

  • Wayback Machine: See archived versions of web pages
  • Archive.today: Snapshot and view saved pages
  • Google Dorks (GHDB): Advanced search query examples
  • Yandex: Alternative search, strong for images

Images and video

  • Google Lens: Reverse image search
  • TinEye: Reverse image search and image origins
  • Yandex Images: Reverse image search, good for faces and places
  • InVID-WeVerify: Video and image verification
  • FotoForensics: Error level analysis of images
  • Jeffrey's EXIF Viewer: Read image metadata

Usernames and email

  • WhatsMyName: Find where a username exists
  • Namechk: Username and domain availability
  • Epieos: Email and phone lookup
  • Have I Been Pwned: Check your email in data breaches
  • Hunter.io: Find company email formats

Domains, IP and networks

  • Who.is: WHOIS lookups
  • ViewDNS.info: DNS, reverse IP and more
  • SecurityTrails: DNS history and subdomains
  • crt.sh: Certificate transparency search
  • URLScan.io: Scan and inspect a URL safely
  • DNSDumpster: Map DNS records and hosts
  • BuiltWith: Technologies a site uses

Devices and infrastructure

  • Shodan: Search internet-connected devices
  • Censys: Hosts, certificates and services
  • GreyNoise: Check if an IP is internet noise
  • VirusTotal: Scan files, URLs and domains

Maps, geolocation and tracking

  • Google Earth: Satellite imagery with history
  • Sentinel Hub EO Browser: Free satellite imagery
  • Overpass Turbo: Query OpenStreetMap data
  • SunCalc: Sun position for dating photos
  • FlightRadar24: Live flight tracking
  • MarineTraffic: Live ship tracking

Social media and people

  • Social Searcher: Search public social posts
  • Wigle: Wi-Fi network map
  • OpenCorporates: Company registry search
  • OCCRP Aleph: Documents and public records

Verification and fact-checking

  • Google Fact Check Explorer: Search published fact checks
  • Hoaxy: Visualize how claims spread
  • Snopes: Fact-check database

Frequently asked questions about OSINT tools

What is OSINT?

OSINT stands for open source intelligence. It means collecting and analyzing information from public sources such as websites, social media, public records, maps and satellite images.

Are these OSINT tools free?

Most tools listed here are free to use in your browser. Some, such as Shodan, Censys and SecurityTrails, need a free account for full features or offer paid plans.

What can I use OSINT tools for?

Common uses are journalism, fact-checking, cybersecurity research, due diligence, and checking your own digital footprint.

Is OSINT legal?

Searching public information is generally legal, but using it to stalk, harass or break privacy laws is not. Always follow the laws and terms of service that apply to you.

Which OSINT tool is best for beginners?

Start with the OSINT Framework to explore categories, then try reverse image search with Google Lens or TinEye and username checks with WhatsMyName.
